SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Simple, consistent presentation rules

Move: latest complete daily observation versus the prior complete observation. Price assets and FX use percentage change; government-bond yield moves use basis points.

1M chart: uses embedded, verified daily OHLC for equities, FX, commodities and crypto; bond charts use daily yield lines. Hover shows exact date and OHLC / price / yield.

Longer ranges: 3M / 6M / YTD / 1Y use verified monthly reference observations. 5Y / MAX use verified annual reference series. The chart automatically switches to Line rather than inventing OHLC candles.

Data convention: the dashboard keeps a single explicit convention inside each horizon and benchmark. Short-horizon closes are primarily checked against Investing.com and market-close reporting from Associated Press (AP) / Reuters. US rates use Federal Reserve / FRED as the official long-run reference; China government-bond yields use ChinaBond / CCDC and PBOC references; long-horizon Brent context uses EIA / IMF reference series. Where official monthly data are averages rather than closes, the chart labels them as reference observations.

Cutoff: the common cross-asset cutoff is 18 Sep 2026, including crypto. This prevents weekend crypto data from being mixed with Friday closes in stocks, FX, commodities and bonds.

Vendor differences: small differences can occur across vendors because of contract, timestamp, quote, or end-of-day conventions. For example, this dashboard’s Brent headline uses the selected ICE/Investing historical-close series at $103.87; narrative and charts stay consistent with that series.

  • Federal Reserve — 16 Sep 2026 FOMC statement

Challenges
  • Different market close times across stocks, FX, commodities, bonds and crypto.
